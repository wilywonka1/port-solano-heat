**Solano Heat** is an original open-world crime game set in Port Solano, a sun-soaked coastal city of downtown towers, suburbs, a beachfront and a marina — built with Three.js and plain static files, no build step and no network requests. You are Juno Vale, newly arrived in town. Run four story missions for fixer Rosa Delgado — a marina courier run, a timed delivery, losing a 3-star heat, and the Marina Score — or free-roam: steal and drive any car, run traffic-dodging streets full of pedestrians, punch or (once unlocked) shoot, collect 10 hidden golden conches, and outrun the Port Solano PD. Stealing, assault and reckless driving raise your wanted level from 1 to 5 stars; police cars pursue and ram, officers on foot join at 3 stars, and getting busted or wasted costs you cash and a trip to the hospital or the station. Progress, cash and position save to your browser automatically.

Controls: WASD move / drive · mouse (pointer lock) look · Shift sprint · E enter/exit car, talk, advance dialogue · Space handbrake · F or click punch · 2 draw pistol · R reload · Esc/P pause. The HUD shows health, cash, wanted stars, weapon and ammo, a speedometer while driving, the current mission objective, and a live minimap with traffic, police, mission markers and conches.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. (Any static file server pointed at `game/` works too — `game/index.html` is the whole game.)
